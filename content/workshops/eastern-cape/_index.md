The Eastern Cape Workshop is a fully funded, week-long, in-person training for nominated lecturers and teaching assistants from Walter Sisulu University, Rhodes University and the University of the Free State.

It builds on the virtual pre-work period, which runs from Monday 16 November 2026 to Friday 22 January 2027 and opens with a live onboarding session on Monday 16 November. The workshop week itself runs from 25 to 29 January 2027, and the venue will be confirmed soon. By the end of the week, participants are ready to adapt and confidently deliver the AI Research Foundations curriculum to their own students.
