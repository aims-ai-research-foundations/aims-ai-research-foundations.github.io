Kenya Workshop 2 is a fully funded, week-long, in-person training for nominated lecturers and teaching assistants from Zetech University, the University of Embu, and Kibabii University, hosted at USIU-Africa in Nairobi.

It builds on the virtual pre-work period, which runs from 1 September to 16 October 2026 and opens with a live onboarding session on Monday 31 August 2026 shared with Kenya Workshop 1. The workshop week itself runs from 19 to 23 October 2026. By the end of the week, participants are ready to adapt and confidently deliver the AI Research Foundations curriculum to their own students.
