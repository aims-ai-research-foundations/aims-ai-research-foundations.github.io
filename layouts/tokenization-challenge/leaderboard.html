Reconstruction is the share of examples where decode(encode(text)) exactly matches the original. Any failure is charged at 3 times the share lost, so a tokenizer that reconstructs every example receives no penalty. The penalty discourages approaches that artificially reduce token counts by modifying or deleting information from the input. Generated from validated submissions in the competition repository. Nothing on this page is entered by hand.
