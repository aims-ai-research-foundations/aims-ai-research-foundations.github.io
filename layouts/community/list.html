About the virtual community cohort

A pan-African community of learners building modern AI research skills.

This virtual cohort brings together learners from across Africa who are passionate about AI research. Together, we learn from a world-class curriculum, support each other's growth, build real projects, and prepare to lead the next wave of AI innovation on the continent.
